Recitals

WHEREAS, the Manufacturer is engaged in the business of producing research-grade compounds and possesses the facilities, expertise, and quality systems necessary to manufacture the Products described herein; and

WHEREAS, the Company wishes to purchase such Products on a research-use-only basis for resale to qualified research buyers, subject to the quality, compliance, and documentation standards set out in this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth below, the Parties agree as follows:

  1. Definitions. “Products” means the research-use-only compounds listed in Schedule A. “COA” means a Certificate of Analysis documenting HPLC purity and Mass Spectrometry identity for a given batch. “Specifications” means the agreed purity, identity, packaging, and labeling requirements for each Product as set out in Schedule A. “Batch” means a defined quantity of a Product produced in a single manufacturing run.
  2. Manufacture & Supply. The Manufacturer shall manufacture and supply the Products to the Company in accordance with the Specifications, applicable law, and the terms of this Agreement. The Manufacturer shall not materially alter the formulation, source materials, or manufacturing process of any Product without the Company’s prior written consent.
  3. Quality & Certificates of Analysis. Every Batch shall be accompanied by a batch-specific COA confirming a minimum purity of [e.g., ≥98%] by HPLC and molecular identity confirmation by Mass Spectrometry. The Company may, at its discretion, submit samples to an independent laboratory (e.g., ACS Labs, Reno, NV) for verification. Product that fails to meet Specifications may be rejected within [NUMBER] days of receipt for replacement or refund.
  4. Orders & Forecasts. The Company shall submit purchase orders specifying Product, quantity, and requested delivery date. The Manufacturer shall confirm or propose alternatives within [NUMBER] business days. No order is binding until accepted in writing by the Manufacturer.
  5. Pricing & Payment. Pricing is set out in Schedule A and may be adjusted only on [NUMBER] days’ written notice. Payment terms are [e.g., Net 30 from invoice date]. All amounts are stated in [CURRENCY].
  6. Shipping, Title & Risk. Delivery terms are [e.g., FOB Manufacturer’s facility]. Title and risk of loss pass to the Company upon [DELIVERY MILESTONE]. The Manufacturer shall package Products to preserve stability and integrity in transit.
  7. Regulatory & Compliance. Each Party shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws governing the manufacture, labeling, sale, and distribution of the Products. All Products shall be labeled and sold strictly for research use only and not for human or veterinary use, diagnostic, or therapeutic purposes. The Manufacturer represents that its facilities meet recognized quality-management standards ([e.g., ISO 9001 / cGMP-aligned]).
  8. Representations & Warranties. The Manufacturer represents and warrants that the Products will (a) conform to the Specifications and applicable COA; (b) be manufactured in compliance with applicable law; and (c) be free of undisclosed contaminants. Each Party warrants that it has full authority to enter into this Agreement.
  9. Intellectual Property. Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property, trademarks, and brand assets. Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party rights in the other’s marks except as expressly stated.
  10. Confidentiality. Each Party shall keep confidential the non-public business, technical, and pricing information of the other and use it solely to perform this Agreement, for a period of [NUMBER] years following disclosure.
  11. Indemnification. Each Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other from third-party claims arising from its own breach of this Agreement, negligence, or violation of law, subject to the limitations in Section 12.
  12. Limitation of Liability. Except for indemnification obligations and breaches of confidentiality, neither Party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Each Party’s aggregate liability shall not exceed [AMOUNT OR FORMULA, e.g., the total amounts paid under this Agreement in the preceding 12 months].
  13. Term & Termination. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of [e.g., 12 months], renewing automatically for successive [e.g., 12-month] terms unless either Party gives [NUMBER] days’ written notice of non-renewal. Either Party may terminate for material breach not cured within [NUMBER] days of written notice.
  14. Force Majeure. Neither Party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, supply disruption, or regulatory action, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate.
  15. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [GOVERNING STATE, e.g., Nevada], without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. The Parties shall attempt good-faith resolution before pursuing [litigation / binding arbitration] in [VENUE].
